Make the things you imagine.

STAR is a local-first AI workspace for Windows. It runs a model on your own hardware, works inside Roblox Studio with your permission, and keeps your chats and projects on your disk.

Download for Windows See the full website

The button opens the latest published release on GitHub, with the Setup file, a portable ZIP and SHA-256 checksums. Builds are not code-signed yet, so compare the checksum before you run one.

Runs locally
STAR Brain installs a verified engine and model, about 3.5 GB, and talks to it over loopback on your PC.
Roblox Studio, on your terms
It connects to the official Studio MCP server. Reading a test place is on by default; editing is a switch you turn on.
Your data stays yours
Chats, models and settings live in %APPDATA%\STAR. There is no account, and updates leave that folder alone.
Early software
Windows 11, 64-bit. Real-device testing is still open, and a small local model makes mistakes.
