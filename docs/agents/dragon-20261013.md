## What was built (all of it ran live on brain 2 through `star_blender`, 40+ tool calls)
Every step went through STAR's own tool (`python -m star.blender_brain2 ... --call`), i.e. `run_bpy` (allow-list checked),
`screenshot`, `render_preview`, `export`. No direct Lab MCP or ssh Python.
- Body + neck + tail + 4 legs: ONE half skeleton of 33 vertices -> Mirror -> Skin -> Subdivision 2, applied.
- Head detail: lower jaw (lofted, Subdivision 2, open mouth, belly-coloured underside), brow ridges, emissive eyes,
  2 pairs of curved horns, 10 fangs, 25 dorsal fin-spikes, 12 claws, a tail spade. Eyes/teeth/spikes/claws are placed
  with ray casts onto the evaluated body surface, so nothing floats.
- Wings: finger bones (upper arm, forearm, 4 fingers, thumb claw) as tapered tubes + a scalloped, slightly billowing
  membrane built as Coons patches between the bones, Solidify (visible from both sides), Mirror, Subdivision 1.
- Belly plates: faces on the spine's underside (by position and normal relative to the nearest spine segment) get
  `Dragon_Belly`, every 0.5 stud of arc length a darker `Dragon_BellyBand` strip (real face materials, so they survive
  into Roblox without textures).
- Rig + idle: `Dragon_Rig` (Body + Wing_Upper/Fore/Hand .L/.R), smooth inverse-distance wing weights, every other part
  skinned 100% to Body; action `Dragon_WingFlap` 48 frames @24 fps (wings up 22 deg / down 26 deg, forearm lag, body bob).
  Checked at frame 1 and 25 (`viewport/front-frame1.png`, `front-frame25.png`).
- Look (renders only): Voronoi scale bump + dark scale edges + darker back (normal-Z ramp), translucent membrane
  (Principled + Translucent mix, alpha 0.93), emissive eyes, dusk world, warm key sun + cool rim sun + fill area, AgX.

## Iterations (art-director passes; images in `iterations/` and `viewport/`)
1. Skin body v1: sauropod, sausage head, thin tubes -> thicker radii, bigger head, S-neck.
2. Head v2/v3: the jaw as a skin branch made a hull "disc" on the head -> jaw moved out as its own loft.
3. Details: eyes protruded like a frog's, brows stuck out like ears -> sunk eyes, smaller brows; wings raised/swept.
4. Pass 1: chest crease at the front-leg branch; tried branch_smoothing 1.0 and an inner leg node.
5. BUG found by looking: those two changes made the Skin modifier's hull fail -> the neck came out as a separate blob
   with a gap (`iterations/05-bug-...png`; spikes floating over the gap). A dedicated leg node with a 0.43 edge made it
   worse. Fixed by going back to the connected layout (front-leg branch on the chest node, branch_smoothing 0.6) with a
   lower first leg node. Lesson: Skin modifier fails silently when radii overlap a short edge; always check side views.
6. Pass 2: belly plates too sparse/patchy -> position + normal rule; eyes glowed white in Eevee (AgX) -> emission 1.5;
   scale texture read as sandpaper -> larger Voronoi cells. Exact boolean union (use_self) of jaw/brows into the
   self-intersecting skin mesh damaged the head -> jaw + brows are JOINED into Dragon_Body as islands instead.

## Honest limits
- Jaw, brows, horns, eyes, teeth, spikes and claws are separate shells sunk into the body (joined per material), not
  boolean-fused: the skin mesh self-intersects at leg junctions; Blender's exact boolean with self-intersection on
  visibly damaged the head, and without it the solver is not reliable on a self-intersecting input (that variant was
  not re-checked after the skin fix). Nothing floats; there are hidden overlaps inside the body.
- Small skin-hull creases remain under the belly at the leg roots (visible only from below).
- Belly plates are face-aligned (stepped edges), not a painted texture.

## What the tool lacked / friction (improvement items) and what I changed in PR #289
Fixed in this branch (with tests, `tests/test_blender_brain2_remote.py`, 85 pass):
1. BUG: any Blender error inside the MCP session came back as "could not be reached (ExceptionGroup)" (anyio wraps the
   LabError) -> unwrapped; the model now sees "Blender code failed: AttributeError: ...". Found on the 2nd call.
2. `screenshot` framing was too loose (a 13-stud dragon filled ~1/3 of the frame) -> `zoom` 0.5-4.
3. `render_preview` framed the bounding SPHERE into the short side: subject tiny -> `zoom` too.
4. No way to look at or render an animation pose -> `frame` (integer, schema-validated) for screenshot/render.
5. `export` dropped rigs and animation (MESH only, bake_anim=False) -> the deforming armature + its action go along
   (FBX MESH+ARMATURE, baked animation; GLB animations), plus `per_mesh_triangles` (Roblox limit is per mesh).
6. `inspect_glb` now reports skins and animations.
7. Screenshots showed bones and light/camera lines -> overlays for bones/extras hidden.
8. CLI: long `run_bpy` JSON broke through cmd/ssh argument quoting -> `--call-file`.
Still open (not changed):
- `MAX_CODE` 12,000 chars: the dragon needed 6 chunks + a shared prelude re-sent each call; a `run_bpy` with a named
  stored library (or 32k) would help.
- RuntimeMaxSec 1800: Blender is killed after 30 min even mid-job; only the last saved state survives (I restarted
  once on purpose). A `keepalive`/extend action, or a status field with seconds left, would help.
- No undo/checkpoint: `run_bpy` saves over the scene; destructive steps (apply modifiers) need a full rebuild. A
  `save_as {scene}` / `checkpoint` action would help.
- No ground/shadow-catcher option and no light/world presets in `render_preview`; the framing ignores lights.
- No close-up framing on one object (`screenshot {object}`), no wireframe/X-ray view for topology review.
- Allow-list: nothing I needed was blocked (bpy.ops.object/mesh/armature/pose, bmesh.ops, modifiers, keyframes all
  fine). Note `bpy.ops.wm` is blocked, so a model cannot save-as/checkpoint itself (by design).
