The track with the higher priority wins on a joint: tracks are evaluated from high to low priority per joint, and once the weights reach 1.0 lower priorities are not evaluated [1]. The order from highest to lowest is Action4, Action3, Action2, Action, Movement, Idle, Core [1][2]. Tracks with the same priority are blended in proportion to their weights [1].
