One ParticleEmitter can emit up to 400 particles per second, and 100 per second on mobile [1]. A particle lives at most 20 s: larger Lifetime values are capped at 20 [1]. Rate is the number of particles emitted every second while the emitter is Enabled [2]; the Emit method creates a given number of particles at once, which suits one-shot bursts [2].
